# Surface Brief: Sterra Weather (index.html)

## Surface Scope & Mode
- **Target:** index.html
- **Mode:** Persuade & Operate (Monolithic Architectural Luxury)
- **Primary Audience:** Connoisseurs of high-end design, architecture, and technology seeking museum-grade meteorological intelligence in an uncompromising black-and-white editorial aesthetic.

## Strategy & Key Interactions
- Pure Black & White Palette: absolute `#000000` / `#0A0A0C` background, crisp pure white `#FFFFFF` typography, muted stone gray `#71717A` for metadata, and stark pure white action elements (`#FFFFFF` buttons with `#000000` text).
- Hero Section: Monolithic architectural opening with large editorial typography ("Atmospheric Telemetry Built to Endure"), stark white CTA button ("Explore Live Weather →"), dramatic grayscale classical monolithic architecture imagery fading seamlessly into the dark background, and an editorial bottom grid.
- Live Weather Deck: Seamlessly reveals the active meteorological console:
  - Top architectural status bar: STERRA monolith wordmark, live coordinates, search field, GPS trigger, and clean unit toggle `[°C / °F]`.
  - Monumental current condition display: massive high-contrast temperature numeral, condition label, local station time, and subtle celestial sun phase arc.
  - Interactive 24-hour temperature spline chart rendered in crisp white curves with hover scrubbers on a hairline grayscale grid.
  - 6-dimension telemetry cards in dark milled granite styling: Humidity, Surface Pressure, Wind Velocity & Vector, UV Index, Optical Visibility, and Dew Point.
  - 7-day extended forecast row with architectural typography, hairline dividers, and high/low temperature metrics.

## Direction contract

### THESIS
Sterra Weather rejects the clutter and pastel consumer tropes of standard weather apps. It elevates meteorology to an architectural luxury art form—strictly black and white, monumental in scale, serene in rhythm, and uncompromising in craft.

### OWN-WORLD
Strict two-tone black and white (`#000000`, `#0A0A0C`, `#18181B`, `#71717A`, `#D4D4D8`, `#FFFFFF`), dramatic grayscale architectural photography, generous negative space, 1px crisp borders (`rgba(255,255,255,0.12)`), razor-sharp typography, and pure white rectangular/pill buttons.

### STORY
The visitor enters an exclusive, architectural digital monolith: a cinematic statement on atmospheric permanence leads directly into a razor-sharp live meteorological console that provides instant global and local weather truth.

### FIRST VIEWPORT
Dramatic split composition: left side features monumental editorial headline, quiet supporting rationale, and pure white button; right side features classical stone architecture emerging from darkness; lower bar anchors with architectural navigation columns.

### FORM
Monolithic Architectural Luxury (Strict Black & White) — Pinned by user reference image, classical architectural drama, expansive negative space, and razor-sharp editorial typography.
